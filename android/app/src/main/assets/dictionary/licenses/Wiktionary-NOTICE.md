Annota modifications: select Mandarin translations, retain translation-specific
sense headings and POS; omit obsolete/rare translations from the query index;
prefer explicitly supplied simplified/traditional pairs' simplified form;
build an independent indexed SQLite database. The preserved source extract and
transformed wiktionary.sqlite are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. They are not
covered by the application's MIT code license. Full source, provenance/hash and
rebuild script are available in the Annota repository's design/dictionary.

Additional extracts retain explicitly attested form_of inflections and standalone
English definitions for heads without a bundled Chinese headword. Nonstandard,
obsolete and cross-reference-only records are filtered. English definitions remain
English and are labelled as such; they are not machine-generated translations.
Morphology and English definitions remain in separate tables of wiktionary.sqlite,
with their original headwords and provenance in sources/kaikki-forms-provenance.json
and sources/kaikki-en-definitions-provenance.json. All derived data above remains
under CC BY-SA 4.0, separately from MIT application code and ECDICT data.
