Stroke Continuity Reconstruction · 笔画连续性重建

Status: preview · version 1.2.1.2 · updated 2026-11-08 cursiveinput lossinterpolationstylus

What it is. Short input gaps inside one stroke are bridged with a curvature-constrained cubic Hermite, never across a pen lift and never into the future.

Problem it solves

Stylus packets are occasionally swallowed (palm/forearm interference, scheduler stalls), which appears as a broken line in fast cursive writing.

How it works

The current packet cadence is compared with a running median to detect a suspicious gap. If bridgeable, a cubic Hermite between the two real endpoints uses tangents from a local three-point osculating-circle/quadratic fit and rejects turns over 70 degrees, curvature over the observed bound or low confidence. Output timestamps stay strictly between the real endpoints.

Difference from existing approaches

Predictors guess the future; this reconstructs only the interior of an observed gap and refuses when the evidence is ambiguous, so it does not invent ink.

Limitations

  • Gaps over 64 ms are refused
  • Never bridges a pen lift or a 121-degree corner
  • Assumes mostly uniform sampling
