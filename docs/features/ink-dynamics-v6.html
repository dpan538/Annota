Ink-Flow Dynamics (inkVersion 6) · 墨流动力学（inkVersion 6）

Status: stable · version 1.2.2 · updated 2026-11-08 fountain peninkdampingdeterministicPDF

What it is. A fixed-step continuous-time model of ink reservoir, flow, paper absorption and nib friction drives the width of new fountain-pen strokes, keeping connected strokes joined and giving slow writing more weight.

Problem it solves

Fixed entry/release tapers and a position-only low-pass filter make connected writing look thin at joins and give little sense of pen-to-paper damping.

How it works

InkDynamics integrates at 1/240 s: pressure drives flow, speed consumes the pressure head, the reservoir refills and drains, absorption and friction respond, and the resulting factors scale width/darkness. A new stroke can start from the previous stroke's state, so a joined-up hand keeps a wet seam. Only new fountain marks use v6; v4 and v5 geometry remain bit-identical.

Difference from existing approaches

Most ink engines change taper parameters or smooth positions. This is a stateful ink-flow model whose damping comes from flow inertia rather than added positional lag, shared by the canvas and PDF exporter.

Limitations

  • Constants are synthetic/emulator-tuned
  • Darkness/edge are exposed but the single-fill renderer currently consumes width only
  • v6 marks are not readable by pre-v6 app versions
